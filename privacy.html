Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

RedVyn ("we", "us") connects patients with eligible blood donors and helps hospitals and blood banks manage their inventory. This policy explains what information we collect through the RedVyn website and apps, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your phone number, name, and the role you sign up as (donor, patient guardian, or hospital / blood bank staff).
  • Donor profile: blood group, date of birth, gender, and the date of your last donation, used to check eligibility.
  • Location: your approximate or precise location, with your permission, so we can match requests with nearby donors.
  • Blood requests: when a guardian raises a case, the patient's blood group, units needed, hospital, and contact details.
  • Hospital records: donation, screening, crossmatch and issue records entered by hospitals and blood banks.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, app version, and a push notification token.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, including verifying your phone number.
  • To match blood requests with eligible donors nearby and notify them.
  • To let donors and guardians contact each other once a donor responds to a request.
  • To help hospitals and blood banks manage donations and inventory.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent misuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Other users, only as needed: a guardian sees a donor's name, blood group and phone number only after that donor responds to their request. Donors see the details of requests sent to them.
  • Hospitals and blood banks involved in your donation or request.
  • Service providers that host our servers, send SMS codes, and deliver push notifications, bound to use the data only on our behalf.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

Health information

Blood group, donation history and screening results are sensitive. We collect only what is needed to check eligibility and support safe transfusions, limit who can see it, and protect it in transit and at rest.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except records that hospitals and blood banks are legally required to keep.

Your choices and rights

  • View and update your profile in the app at any time.
  • Turn off location access or notifications in your device settings.
  • Pause donor availability, or delete your account from the app or by contacting us.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it.

Children

RedVyn is not intended for anyone under 18. Donors must meet the minimum age for blood donation. Patient details for minors are provided by their guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make significant changes, we will update the date above and let you know in the app.
